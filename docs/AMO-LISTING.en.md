# Text for the Mozilla listing

## Name

FireUp

## Summary

Reduce Firefox's memory use by unloading idle tabs. Keep recent tabs and the sites you choose loaded.

## Description

Keep your tabs open without keeping every page loaded. FireUp unloads idle tabs to reduce Firefox's memory use. They stay in the tab bar and reload when you return to them.

By default, your 10 most recently used tabs stay loaded. Other tabs can be unloaded after 10 minutes of inactivity. You can change both settings.

You can also:

- Unload tabs yourself: selected tabs, the current window, all windows or a particular site.
- Protect a tab temporarily or until you remove its protection.
- Exclude websites you want to keep loaded.
- Search and filter your tabs.
- Choose a light, dark or system theme.
- Export your settings and preview an import before applying it.

If you quickly return to a tab that FireUp unloaded, it waits longer before unloading that tab again.

Active tabs in each window, pinned tabs and tabs playing audio stay loaded. FireUp also skips camera, microphone and screen sharing when Firefox reports them. Private tabs are excluded.

Protect tabs with unsaved work, editors, forms or silent playback. FireUp doesn't read page contents and can't reliably recognize all of these situations. Unloading a tab may lose unsaved work.

Tab information is processed locally and isn't sent to the developer. There are no analytics, ads or external services.

The interface supports English and Russian. It starts with your browser's language, and you can change it in settings.

Requires desktop Firefox 140 or later. Memory savings depend on the pages and Firefox.
